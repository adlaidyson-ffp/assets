Welcome to my custom-designed weather app for Upper Haugh and the surrounding areas of Rawmarsh. It provides hourly, daily, and weekly forecasts, along with detailed information cards covering precipitation, humidity, wind, air quality (AQI index), UV index, and both sunrise/sunset and moonrise/moonset times. Any weather warnings issued by the Met Office will also appear in a card.

The web version can be installed as a Progressive Web App (PWA), allowing you to add it to your home screen and use it just like a native app.

All data is retrieved from a custom backend (uhwappapi.adlaidyson.co.uk/weather), which sources weather information from the OpenWeatherMap API. In testing, it’s proven to be more accurate than many popular apps, such as Apple Weather and BBC Weather.

Future updates will include the ability to expand each day within the weekly forecast, plus official native apps for iOS/iPadOS, Android, Windows, and macOS, available through their respective app stores — and more exciting features to come!
